# ✨ Extraordinary Moment Finder

Extraordinary Moment Finder is an AI-powered experience designer that helps you create meaningful, memorable moments—based on who you are, not just where you're going.

Instead of generic itineraries or “top 10” lists, this app focuses on:

- Personalized experiences that fit your vibe
- Turning inspiration into real-life plans
- Building around what you already have booked
- Creating story-worthy moments, not just activities

## 🚀 What it does

- Suggests curated experiences for a single destination
- Builds loose, realistic day plans for cruise or multi-stop itineraries
- Incorporates inspiration from TikTok, Instagram, or anywhere else
- Adapts to your group, energy, and preferences
- Keeps things flexible, human, and not overplanned

## 💡 Philosophy

This app is built on a simple idea:

> Experiences don’t have to be perfect to be meaningful.

It helps you:
- enjoy the moment
- stay flexible
- create memories that feel real—not forced
